02

Working prototype

All three modes, functioning. The tracing check is the real algorithm — draw inside the letter and it passes, scribble and it fails. Audio here uses the browser's voice for demo purposes; the shipped app plays pre-recorded WAV files of a local Marathi speaker from the APK.

ModeWhat it actually does
ऐकाPlays the target sound, renders four tiles from the confusion table, scores the tap. Correct tile turns turmeric; a wrong tap dims and the sound replays automatically — no penalty, no dead end.
बोलाIn the shipped app this runs the Marathi IndicConformer CTC head locally and scores the recording against the on-screen candidate set. The browser cannot host a 130 MB ONNX model, so what runs here is the capture half — mic permission, live amplitude, silence detection, and the three verdict bands. The scoring itself is in pron_score.py and PronunciationChecker.kt.
लिहाRasterises the target glyph to an offscreen canvas, then compares finger strokes against it: coverage (how much of the letter you covered) and spill (how much you drew outside it). Pass is coverage ≥ 70% with spill ≤ 25%.
Try the failure paths too. In ऐका, tap a wrong tile. In लिहा, scribble across the whole box — high coverage, high spill, and it correctly fails. That asymmetry is why coverage alone is not enough.

03

Mode functionality

Each mode is one input, one validator, one verdict. Everything below runs on-device with no model download; the total added APK weight is audio assets plus a stroke-template JSON.

१ · ऐका

sound → shape · recognition

Prompt
Plays audio/mr/ka.wav. Nothing is shown until the sound finishes, so the child must listen rather than pattern-match.
Input
One tap out of four tiles. Tiles are 72dp tall, spaced 11dp, all within thumb reach on a 5" screen.
Validator
String equality. No ML, no ambiguity, no false negatives.
Distractors
Pulled from a hand-built confusion table: visually similar (क/फ, घ/ध, ब/व), then acoustically similar (त/ट, स/श). Stored as confusions.json.
Pass rule
4 of 5 items correct advances the rung. 2 or fewer repeats the same rung with fresh distractors.
Wrong answer
Tile dims, sound replays once, child taps again. Never a red X, never a score deduction, never a blocking modal.
Offline cost
~40 KB of audio per letter at 22 kHz mono Opus. Full Varnamala plus 200 words fits under 12 MB.

२ · बोला

shape → sound · on-device asr

Model
indicconformer_stt_mr_hybrid_ctc_rnnt_large (AI4Bharat, MIT). 120M-param Conformer encoder, CTC head only. INT8 ONNX ≈ 120–130 MB, bundled in the APK. Never downloaded, never called out.
Runtime
ONNX Runtime Mobile, 2 threads, CPU. Export with NeMo's mel preprocessor fused into the graph so Android feeds raw 16 kHz samples — a Kotlin reimplementation of log-mel is the classic way this pipeline silently breaks.
Scoring · 1
Closed-set CTC. Compute log P(word | audio) for the target and the 3 words that were on screen, length-normalised, and take the argmax. Accent shifts every candidate together, so the ranking survives it. Free decoding plus string comparison does not.
Scoring · 2
Per-akshara GOP. Viterbi-align the target against the CTC posteriors, then score each aligned segment against the frame's best token. This is what lets the screen say "the ळ" instead of showing a cross.
Bands
Three, never four. accepted — target wins by margin ≥ 0.35 and min GOP ≥ −1.2. retry — everything else. no_speech — peak below the noise floor, reported as a mic problem.
Never blocks
After the second attempt the item is accepted regardless of score. A mis-scoring model must be able to annoy a child, never to stop them.
Calibration
The two thresholds are the whole ballgame and cannot be set from a desk. Fix them on Palghar recordings in your Week 13 field study, optimising false-reject rate at a fixed false-accept rate — a child wrongly told they are wrong is the expensive error.
Data
Audio stays in memory and is discarded on screen exit. Inference is local, so §11.4 holds unchanged.

३ · लिहा

sound → stroke · production

Prompt
The letter's sound plays; a faint ghost glyph and numbered start dots appear. Ghost can be turned off for a mastered letter, which converts tracing into free writing.
Input
Finger strokes on a canvas. Each stroke is captured as a point list with timestamps.
Validator
A chamfer distance transform of the glyph, built once per letter, then five checks: scribble (drawn length ≤ 2.5× glyph skeleton), spill (≤ 25% of ink beyond 1.0× pen width), shirorekha (top bar ≥ 60% covered), region floor (every part ≥ 55%), coverage (≥ 70% within 1.75× pen width).
Why asymmetric
Generous about a child missing the line — cheap digitisers and small fingers wander. Strict about drawing where no letter is. Symmetric tolerances passed a full-canvas scribble at 22% spill in testing.
Why the extra gates
Because a global average hides a missing part. Omitting the शिरोरेखा scores 76%; bar-and-stem-only scores 72%. Both read as correct on coverage alone. The per-region floor is the general fix; the shirorekha gate exists so the feedback can name it.
Resolution
Every tolerance derives from the glyph's own pen width (2× the 90th-percentile inner distance), so one threshold set works from 480×854 to 1080p with no retuning.
Wrong answer
Named in speech and Marathi text, not scored: "वरची रेघ राहिली", "अजून थोडं राहिलं", "रेषेच्या आत लिही". Weakest quadrant highlighted. Then "पुसा" and retry.
Offline cost
Zero models, zero assets. Distance transform is two O(pixels) passes — within 0.71 px of exact Euclidean, well inside the tolerances it feeds, and fast enough for 60 fps on a 1 GB device.
What still needs deciding, and what does not. D-02 in §16 can now be closed on the speech side: the Marathi IndicConformer is MIT-licensed, runs offline, and INT8 exports for all 22 languages are already published, so nothing needs training. What cannot be closed from a desk is the pair of thresholds. IndicConformer is trained largely on adult speech, and Warli-accented children in a room with forty other children are the hardest case it will ever see — so the accept margin and GOP floor must come out of your Week 13 field recordings, and until they do, ship with the bands set generously and the two-attempt accept rule in place. Handwriting is the part with no off-the-shelf answer: the geometric check in Mode 3 needs no model at all, so recommend re-scoping the handwriting half of D-02 to geometric validation and spending the ML budget on speech.
